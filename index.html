Tune Workshop synth STEP ONE Put your music in Drop a whole zip or any number of loose files. Everything is read here in the browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere. Every tune that arrives with a melody gets its skeleton, outline and full version built automatically on the way in. You adjust the corners by ear afterwards — you never start from nothing. Drop files here or Choose files WHAT EACH FORMAT GIVES YOU format what comes out .mid .midi the melody, complete — notes become numbers automatically .xml .musicxml .mxl the melody, complete, plus the written key .abc the melody, complete, plus the key and metre .tef title, composer, notes, instruments and tunings — but not the melody (see below) .json a Workshop or Sets of Eight backup, read straight back in TablEdit's .tef is a closed binary format. The Workshop reads everything around the notes — which is enough to build your catalogue — but the notes themselves have to come out of TablEdit as MIDI.
