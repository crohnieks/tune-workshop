What each format gives you
formatwhat comes out
.mid .midithe melody, complete — notes become numbers automatically
.xml .musicxml .mxlthe melody, complete, plus the written key
.abcthe melody, complete, plus the key and metre
.teftitle, composer, notes, instruments and tunings — but not the melody (see below)
.jsona Workshop or Sets of Eight backup, read straight back in

TablEdit's .tef is a closed binary format. The Workshop reads everything around the notes — which is enough to build your catalogue — but the notes themselves have to come out of TablEdit as MIDI. The instructions that came with this file explain the one-time export.
